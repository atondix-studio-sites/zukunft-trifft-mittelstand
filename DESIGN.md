# Design direction

The visitor is a teacher planning careers guidance at a school desk, or a local employer looking for apprenticeship contacts during the working day. Clear navigation and human encounters lead the page.

Voice: approachable, practical, local. Keep the established Barlow Semi Condensed and Source Sans 3 families and navy / green / lime palette. The reference is a regional encounter campaign: photographs of conversations, confident solid color, clear language and useful routes for two audiences.

The homepage follows the decision: understand the format, see an encounter, choose school or business, understand the sequence, make contact. Avoid repeated icon cards, decorative section labels, fake statistics and placeholder portraits. School and business options are parallel but use distinct surfaces. Numbering belongs only to actual event sequences.

The user prefers the original icon-led benefits band and process sequence. Preserve these as a deliberate part of the identity: lime duotone icons on navy for benefits, green duotone icons with readable step numbers for the process. Use open columns instead of cards, and remove directional connectors when columns wrap. On phones, benefits become compact icon-and-text rows.

Photographs are generated illustrative scenes with visible captions; they must never be attached to a testimonial as if they depicted its author. Real event photography can replace them once approved. Responsive images reserve their space and use Next image optimization. Motion enhances visible content and respects reduced motion.

Headings use balanced wrapping and a maximum of 4.8rem on the homepage. Body copy uses dark readable tones. Navigation identifies the current page. Controls have visible keyboard focus and touch-sized targets.
